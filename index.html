Quick reference

  • Rice: Arborio, Carnaroli or Vialone Nano for traditional-style risotto.
  • Butterfly whisk: never above speed 4, on every model (TM5, TM6, TM7, TM31).
  • TM6 at 95 °C / 200 °F or above: simmering basket on the lid instead of the black measuring cup. Do not copy this rule to other models.
  • Bowl limit: 2.2 L max on TM6 and TM7; TM5 and TM31 follow the fill marking in their manuals.
  • No universal ratio: trial stock-to-rice points are 2.5:1, 3:1 and 3.5:1 (mL stock per g dry rice). Test, then record.
  • Judge after resting: check grains and sauce separately, and again at 3, 5 and 10 minutes.
  • Chicken add-in: 165 °F / 74 °C internal, checked with a thermometer, not the timer.
  • Leftovers: chill within 1 hour, eat within 24 hours, reheat once until steaming hot.

Rinsing: many traditional recipes use unrinsed risotto rice. One study found washing had no large effect on texture in the rice it tested, but it was not a Thermomix risotto comparison. Pick one routine and keep it the same every batch.

Keep total contents under your model’s maximum fill mark. Halving or doubling needs a fresh liquid and cooking check.

Fix it: symptom → cause → remedy

Rule one: assess grains and sauce separately before adding stock or cooking longer.
